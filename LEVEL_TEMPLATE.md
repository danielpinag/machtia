## The scale

Five levels per area, named in Nahuatl, mapped to the Dreyfus model of skill
acquisition. The recorded level is always the one **demonstrated** in the
Evidence log, never the one claimed.

| # | Name | Dreyfus | At this level you… |
|---|------|---------|--------------------|
| 1 | **Yancuic** ("new one") | Novice | Follow recipes step by step; can't yet diagnose why a deviation broke things. |
| 2 | **Momachtiani** ("one who learns") | Advanced Beginner | Recognize recurring patterns from experience, but still treat guidelines as absolute rules. |
| 3 | **Toltecatl** ("skilled craftsman") | Competent | Plan deliberately, choose between approaches for stated reasons, troubleshoot by reasoning. |
| 4 | **Temachtiani** ("one who teaches") | Proficient | See the system as a whole, anticipate failure modes, and can teach the topic and defend its tradeoffs. |
| 5 | **Tlamatini** ("sage") | Expert | Operate on intuition built from deep experience; know when the rules don't apply and create new approaches. |

An area with no evidence in 90+ days is flagged **rusty 🕸**: the level stays,
but explanations recalibrate one step down until it is re-touched.
